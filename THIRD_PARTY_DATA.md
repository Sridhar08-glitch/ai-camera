# THIRD-PARTY TRAINING DATA — ATTRIBUTION & LICENSE NOTICES

This file records attribution for third-party datasets used (or proposed for use) to
train this project's detection models. Giving credit here does **not** imply the
dataset creators endorse or own this project's software or trained model weights — it
satisfies the datasets' attribution licenses (CC BY / CC BY-SA).

**Clear separation of ownership:**
- **Dataset creator** — the organization credited below (owns the images/annotations).
- **Architecture/software** — FCOS is from torchvision (BSD-3); PyTorch (BSD-style).
- **This project's trained weights + ONNX artifact** — produced by our own training run
  from **random initialization** (no third-party pretrained weights). They are our artifact.
- **This project's application** — our software.

Every dataset below independently passed the production-training rights gate (or is
proposed to, pending explicit human approval).

## UVH-26 (Urban Vision Hackathon Dataset) — CC BY 4.0
- **Creator:** Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIM) @ Indian Institute of
  Science (IISc), Bengaluru.
- **Official source:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/iisc-aim/UVH-26 (official publisher release)
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**) — commercial use
  permitted **with attribution**. (Verified: `license: cc-by-4.0` in the official repo.)
- **Content:** ~26,646 1080p frames from ~2,800 Bengaluru Safe City CCTV cameras; ~1.8M
  bounding boxes; 14 fine-grained India-specific vehicle classes. Faces and license plates
  masked by the creators.
- **Technical report / citation:** *The Urban Vision Hackathon Dataset and Models: Towards
  Image Annotations and Accurate Vision Models for Indian Traffic* — arXiv:2511.02563.
- **License re-verified live 2026-07-16:** `license: cc-by-4.0` in the official repo. The
  actual COCO annotation JSON was downloaded (`UVH-26-Val/UVH-26-MV-Val.json`, 16 MB) and its
  `categories` confirm the real 14-class taxonomy: Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, MUV, Bus, Truck,
  Three-wheeler, Two-wheeler, LCV, Mini-bus, Tempo-traveller, Bicycle, Van, Others. Per-image
  `license` field present. Mapped to canonical via `class_mapping_version=uvh_bmd_5class_v1`
  (Hatchback/Sedan/SUV/MUV→CAR, Bus/Mini-bus→BUS, Truck→TRUCK, Two-wheeler→MOTORCYCLE,
  Bicycle→BICYCLE; Three-wheeler/LCV/Tempo-traveller/Van/Others **excluded**, never force-mapped).
  **PEDESTRIAN has no examples in these vehicle-only datasets → the V1 model is 5-class.**
- **Modifications by this project (if used):** class remap to canonical 5-class taxonomy;
  letterbox preprocessing; leakage-safe re-split; conversion to canonical COCO. Original
  annotations are preserved immutably.
- **Access version/date:** UVH-26-v1.0, accessed 2026-07-16.
